TLDR: Generic retention campaigns fail because they guess when customers are ready to buy. Stamped Lifecycle automates personalized messages triggered by six key “Moments”—Cross-Sell, Replenishment, Late Replenishment, Sample Upsell, Subscription Upsell, and Lapse Prevention. Each Moment uses real purchase behavior to reach customers when they’re actually ready, not on arbitrary timelines.
